# Peajes Data Privacy & Integrity Validator

## Objetivo
Enforce strict data validation rules, ensure minimal disclosure of PII through masking and tokenization, and verify adherence to logging standards and data format conventions (UUIDs, ISO 8601) across all data models and processing layers.
