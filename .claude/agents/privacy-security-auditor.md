# Sensitive Data & PCI DSS Compliance Auditor

## Objetivo
Ensure strict adherence to data privacy laws (Ley 1581/2012) and PCI DSS Level 4 controls, including PII masking, PAN tokenization, secure data transmission (HTTPS/TLS 1.3), restricted access, encrypted storage, and comprehensive audit logging with PII exclusion.
